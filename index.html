About Destination Consulting

Frontline experience. Applied research. Community roots.

Based in New York's Capital Region, Destination Consultation LLC brings together direct experience in hospital-linked violence intervention, inpatient behavioral health, and maternal and child health with doctoral-level applied research on resilience in underserved communities. We help organizations translate lived and clinical experience into programs that are fundable, sustainable, and genuinely responsive to the communities they serve.

  • Direct frontline background in community violence intervention (CVI) and behavioral health
  • Doctoral research on transgenerational resilience in underserved communities
  • Deep familiarity with New York State grant, RFP, and prequalification processes
  • Positioned as an MWBE-track consulting partner for larger primes and nonprofits alike
